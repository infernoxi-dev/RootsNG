THE LITTLE DETAILS

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to help you plan your visit to Roots Restaurant & Café.

What time are you open?

Our listed general opening hours are 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Please contact your preferred branch to confirm any holiday or special-hour changes.

Where can I find Roots?

Roots has branches in Aba, Enugu, and Umuahia. Visit our Locations page for branch addresses and contact numbers.

Can I book a table?

Yes, you can use the Reservations page to prepare a booking request. This recreated website is a front-end demo, so the request is not automatically sent to the restaurant. Please call your branch to confirm.

Where can I see the menu and prices?

Browse our Menu page for food categories. The dishes shown in this recreation are illustrative and may not reflect the live menu or current prices. Contact your branch for the latest menu.

Do you cater for events or group visits?

Please contact your preferred branch to ask about group visits, celebrations, or event arrangements.

How do I share feedback?

Use the Contact Us page or email customercare@rootsng.com. For an urgent issue, contact your nearest branch directly.

How can I apply for a job?

Visit the Careers page to see how to enquire about opportunities with the Roots team.

Still have a question?

Our team can help with branch details, bookings, and other enquiries.
